The slicer it is built on

OPF's slicer is a fork of OrcaSlicer. Its Prepare and Preview, the slicing engine, the printer and filament profiles and most of the program are their work. Each project in the line is licensed under the GNU AGPL 3.0, and OPF keeps their credits in its About box.

The slicer also includes the libraries OrcaSlicer is built with, among them wxWidgets, Boost, CGAL, OpenVDB, Eigen and libcurl, each under its own licence. They are listed, with their licences, in the slicer's source.

The farm it is built on

The farm program runs on Electron (MIT), which includes Chromium (BSD and others) and Node.js (MIT); its licence files are in the farm folder of the installation. Inside it are React, three.js, MQTT.js, basic-ftp, busboy and fflate, all under the MIT licence.

The fonts are Barlow by Jeremy Tribby and JetBrains Mono by JetBrains, both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, served by this site itself.

Talking to Anycubic printers over the local network follows what the community worked out in anycubic_ha_local and kobra-connect; Bambu Lab's local protocol follows its published LAN mode and the community's notes on it.

If you want the source of any part of OPF and cannot get it from the links above, open an issue on GitHub and we will send it.
