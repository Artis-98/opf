Changelog

Release notes, improvements and fixes.

0.2.36 November 2026current

A calmer startup, clearer updates, and the right status after a stopped print.

  • Printing means printing. A stale Stopped flag no longer hides a new print when the printer reports it is preparing or printing.
  • A loading wheel while OPF starts. Farm and Timeline keep connection-error pages hidden while the farm starts and reconnects.
  • Quieter starts and updates. Windows launchers hide their command windows, and an installed slicer starts its bundled farm ahead of an old development launcher.
  • Clearer farm updates. The update button shows when a download is in progress, then offers Restart. The farm reconnects even after a very quick restart, and its running version is shown in the top bar.
0.2.26 November 2026

Stop asks what comes next, OPF updates itself in small steps, and your printers look like your printers.

  • Stop asks: print it again, or cancel. Stopping a print, in OPF or on the printer's own screen, no longer leaves the printer stuck as Error. OPF asks whether to print the plate again or cancel it; then you take the parts off, and the plate starts again or the next one does.
  • Small updates by themselves. An installed OPF now downloads changes to the farm by itself, a few megabytes instead of the whole installer, and offers Update ready, Restart. Every update is signed and checked, and if one ever fails to start, OPF goes back to the version it was installed with.
  • Your printers as they look. Every printer shows a picture of its model, on the shelves, in the printer panel, on the Timeline and in Add a printer, with its AMS colours beside it.
  • Sending, and how far it got. A big plate takes a minute or two to reach the printer; its card now says Sending with the percent, and the start buttons wait.
  • Green, also where 0.2.0 saved teal. A model OPF 0.2.0 saved in OrcaSlicer's teal turns Bambu green.
0.2.117 October 2026

Printers found on your network, added once, and every plate sent in one go.

  • Add a printer finds your printers. It looks on your network as it opens: Bambu Lab printers with their name, model, address and serial number, Anycubic Kobra printers in LAN mode with their model and address. Pick one; for a Bambu printer only the access code from its screen is left to type.
  • A printer is added once. No setup wizard on first start: a printer added in the Farm tab gets its printer profile and filaments in Prepare by itself.
  • Send all plates. Arrange a big job over many plates and send them all at once: OPF slices what is not sliced yet and puts each plate in a queue, on the printer you picked or, with none picked, on whichever is free first. The print button now offers Send plate, Send all plates and the three exports, and names the printer they go to, such as Send all plates to Mirdza; printing past the farm is gone.
  • Bambu green in place of OrcaSlicer's teal, also on the model, the object box and notifications, and OPF in the window title.
  • Sync from the farm. The Sync buttons fill the filament list with what is loaded in the printer picked in the Farm tab, its colours and materials, and Bambu's exact filament where the printer reports it.
  • Textured PEI is the plate new projects start on.
  • Add a printer starts empty, with grey hints, so the model list shows every printer; picking a model fills in the connection.
  • OPF's logo in the setup wizard and on the slicer's pages, and OPF's own site, here, at opf.iiteg.com.
0.2.016 October 2026

The first OPF you can install: one installer for the slicer and the farm.

  • One installer puts in OPF Slicer and the farm. Open OPF from the Start menu; the farm starts with it and stays by the clock.
  • Before each print: bed levelling, flow calibration, resonance compensation and timelapse, ticked per printer.
  • Plates named after their parts, not Untitled.
  • One window. Open OPF in the tray brings the open slicer forward.
  • OPF everywhere: the program is OPF.exe, the icon is the shelf on a white tile so it shows on a dark taskbar, and the slicer says OPF where it said OrcaSlicer, its credits kept. OPF keeps its own settings, so an installed OrcaSlicer is left alone.
  • Updates: an installed OPF checks for a new release every few hours and offers it.
0.114 October 2026

The first version, run from its source on the farm it was made for.

  • The farm: printers on shelves, a queue for each, Bed is clear, start next, Duplicate, and Edit to open a queued plate again.
  • The Timeline: the next 48 hours of every printer, with working hours.
  • OPF Slicer: OrcaSlicer with Farm and Timeline tabs, Send to farm, and Bambu green.
  • Printers: Bambu Lab over the local network, and Anycubic Kobra 3 and S1 in LAN mode.
  • The small OPF by the clock: every printer at a glance, a note when a print finishes, and it starts with Windows.
