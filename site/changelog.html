0.2.116 October 2026current

Printers found on your network, added once, and every plate sent in one go.

  • Add a printer finds your printers. It looks on your network as it opens: Bambu Lab printers with their name, model, address and serial number, Anycubic Kobra printers in LAN mode with their model and address. Pick one; for a Bambu printer only the access code from its screen is left to type.
  • A printer is added once. No setup wizard on first start: a printer added in the Farm tab gets its printer profile and filaments in Prepare by itself.
  • Send all plates. Arrange a big job over many plates and send them all at once: OPF slices what is not sliced yet and puts each plate in a queue, on the printer you picked or, with none picked, on whichever is free first. The print button now offers Send plate, Send all plates and the three exports; printing past the farm is gone.
  • Sync from the farm. The Sync buttons fill the filament list with what is loaded in the printer picked in the Farm tab, its colours and materials, and Bambu's exact filament where the printer reports it.
  • Textured PEI is the plate new projects start on.
  • Add a printer starts empty, with grey hints, so the model list shows every printer; picking a model fills in the connection.
  • OPF's logo in the setup wizard and on the slicer's pages, and OPF's own site, here, at opf.iiteg.com.
0.2.016 October 2026

The first OPF you can install: one installer for the slicer and the farm.

  • One installer puts in OPF Slicer and the farm. Open OPF from the Start menu; the farm starts with it and stays by the clock.
  • Before each print: bed levelling, flow calibration, resonance compensation and timelapse, ticked per printer.
  • Plates named after their parts, not Untitled.
  • One window. Open OPF in the tray brings the open slicer forward.
  • OPF everywhere: the program is OPF.exe, the icon is the shelf on a white tile so it shows on a dark taskbar, and the slicer says OPF where it said OrcaSlicer, its credits kept. OPF keeps its own settings, so an installed OrcaSlicer is left alone.
  • Updates: an installed OPF checks for a new release every few hours and offers it.
0.114 October 2026

The first version, run from its source on the farm it was made for.

  • The farm: printers on shelves, a queue for each, Bed is clear, start next, Duplicate, and Edit to open a queued plate again.
  • The Timeline: the next 48 hours of every printer, with working hours.
  • OPF Slicer: OrcaSlicer with Farm and Timeline tabs, Send to farm, and Bambu green.
  • Printers: Bambu Lab over the local network, and Anycubic Kobra 3 and S1 in LAN mode.
  • The small OPF by the clock: every printer at a glance, a note when a print finishes, and it starts with Windows.
